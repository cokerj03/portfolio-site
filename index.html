About

For more than ten years I kept complicated operations running — coordinating freight across land, air, and sea, guiding thousands of prospective students through enrollment, training new hires, and fixing problems in real time when a shipment or a system didn't behave. Somewhere in the middle of all that, I got curious about the systems underneath the work, and started building them instead of just running them.

I'm finishing a B.S. in Computer Science at Concordia University – St. Paul, with graduate work in Cloud Computing and Data Science & AI underway at Campbellsville University. The three projects below are that combination put to work: operations experience that knows what a real workflow needs, paired with the engineering to build it.

I currently work remotely as a Student Engagement Specialist, building Tableau/Excel reporting and managing Salesforce CRM data integrity for a higher-ed enrollment team — the same domain the Enrollment Analytics project below is drawn from.
